# Editorial Newsroom Cold Pitch & Distribution Playbook
**Framework:** High-Value Empirical Data Journalism Pitches  
**Target:** 10 Licensed Publications in 60 Days ($8,000–$25,000/mo Retainer Run Rate)

---

## Phase 1: Identifying High-Probability Newsroom Desks (Days 1–15)
1. **Target Editors:** Health tech editors, data journalism desk leads, healthcare enterprise reporters.
2. **The High-Density Hook:** Pitch the empirical data divergence first, not the general topic (e.g., "Exclusive Benchmark: Why serverless edge nodes drop clinical API latencies from 185ms to 8ms").
3. **The Proof Asset:** Attach an interactive SVG chart preview rather than a generic text pitch.

---

## Phase 2: Frictionless CMS Integration (Days 16–30)
1. Offer the sub-10ms edge embed snippet: `<div class="data-journalism-embed">`.
2. Demonstrate that zero local engineering overhead is required by the publisher.
3. Provide JSON-LD structured data for instant Google News / SEO indexing.

---

## Phase 3: Converting One-Offs to Monthly Syndication Retainers (Days 31–60)
1. Following successful publication of Story 1, deliver performance analytics on time-on-page and engagement.
2. Present the Monthly 4-Pack ($800/mo) or Executive Publisher Retainer ($2,500/mo).
